## What is Infra Insight

Infra Insight is my public infrastructure notebook: networking, virtualization, storage, and everything needed to keep services running reliably.

I write about what I build, configure, and break at home, in a lab built from hardware I already had. The idea is that anyone reading can recreate each guide, so I document the steps, the commands, and—above all—what went wrong along the way. Failures teach more than tutorials that work on the first try.

## What you will find here

- **Homelab:** building an environment with Proxmox, TrueNAS, and Cisco switches, step by step
- **Networking:** VLANs, trunks, firewalls, and the reasoning behind the decisions
- **Storage and backups:** ZFS, NFS, drive testing, and backup strategies
- **Field notes:** real errors, diagnostics, and lessons learned

## About me

I'm Nuno. I have worked in IT and infrastructure for over 20 years, with experience in network maintenance, VMware virtual servers, Veeam backups, PRTG monitoring, and managing Check Point and Palo Alto firewalls, including multi-site production firewall replacements.

I have a particular passion for networking, and this lab is my way to experiment, learn, and practice away from production environments.

## The lab

This lab has one rule: **zero cost**. I spend nothing on buying hardware or software. I use equipment I already had at home and free tools, to show that you can learn and experiment seriously without spending money.

The lab lives in a wooden rolling cabinet I am building myself, and all the gear is named after places from the Harry Potter universe: `hogwarts` (the hypervisor), `gringotts` (the storage), and so on. You can follow the build in the posts of the series.
